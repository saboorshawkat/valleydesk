FAQ
Common questions
Is Valley Desk an official government website?
No. Valley Desk is an independent portal that aggregates and verifies publicly available recruitment information for Jammu & Kashmir. Always confirm details on the linked official website before applying or paying any fee.
How do I report an outdated or wrong notification?
Use the form above, choose "Report an incorrect / expired notification," and include the job title plus a link to the correct official source if you have one. We usually correct it within hours.
Can I suggest a department or job category to add?
Absolutely — select "Suggest a job listing we've missed" in the form, or message us on Telegram/WhatsApp directly.
Do you charge anything for job alerts or tools?
No. Valley Desk, its job feed, and all browser-based tools are completely free and always will be.
