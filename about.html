Our Story
Built Because Searching For A Job In J&K Shouldn't Feel Like A Full Time Job Itself

Valley Desk Started With A Simple Frustration: Notifications Scattered Across Dozens Of Telegram Channels, WhatsApp Forwards And Department Websites. Half Of Them Outdated, Most Without Any Way To Tell What's Actually Relevant To You.

We Set Out To Fix That With One Clean, Fast, Mobile Friendly Feed For Every Govt Recruitment Notification, Exam Result And Admit Card That Matters To Jammu & Kashmir Aspirants. JKSSB, JKPSC, JK Police, J&K Bank, Judiciary, Teaching, Health & University Jobs, All In One Place.

Everything Is Checked Against Official Sources Before It's Published, Updated The Moment Something Changes And Organised So You Can Filter To Exactly The Department Or Category You're Applying For. No More Scrolling Through Unrelated Posts To Find The One Notification You Actually Need.
