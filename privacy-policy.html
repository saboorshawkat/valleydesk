This Privacy Policy explains how Valley Desk (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses, and protects information when you visit valleydesk.in. By using the Service, you agree to the practices described below.

Interpretation & Definitions

Interpretation

Words that begin with a capital letter have a specific meaning under this Privacy Policy, whether they're written in singular or plural form.

Definitions

  • Company ("Valley Desk", "We", "Us" or "Our") refers to Valley Desk, the operator of this website.
  • Service refers to the Valley Desk website, accessible from valleydesk.in.
  • You means the person visiting or using the Service.
  • Personal Data means any information that relates to an identifiable individual, such as a name or email address.
  • Usage Data means data collected automatically as you browse — things like pages visited, time spent, and device details.
  • Cookies are small files placed on your device by a website to remember information about your visit.
  • Country refers to Jammu & Kashmir, India.

Data We Collect

Valley Desk is a job-listing portal, not a social platform — we collect very little personal information, and only what's needed to run the site properly.

Information you give us

  • If you reach out through our Contact Us form, we receive whatever you type in: your name, email address, and message.
  • We do not currently require account creation to browse job listings, results, or admit cards.

Information collected automatically

  • Standard usage data such as your approximate location (city/region level), browser type, device type, pages viewed, and time spent on the site.
  • Your IP address, which is logged automatically by our hosting and analytics providers for security and performance purposes.

Cookies

We use a small number of cookies to remember your theme preference (light/dark mode) and to power basic site analytics. You can disable cookies from your browser settings at any time — the site will still work, though your theme preference may not be remembered between visits.

How We Use Your Information

Any data we collect is used only to:

  • Operate, maintain, and improve the Service — for example, understanding which job categories are most viewed.
  • Respond to messages sent through our Contact Us form.
  • Detect, prevent, and address technical issues, spam, or abuse.
  • Analyse traffic trends so we can prioritise which departments or categories to track more closely.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

Sharing Your Information

We may share limited data in the following situations:

  • With service providers — such as our hosting provider or analytics tools, strictly to help us run the Service.
  • For legal reasons — if required by applicable law or a valid request from a government or judicial authority.
  • To protect our rights — to investigate and prevent fraud, abuse, or security issues affecting the Service.

Data Retention

We retain the limited data we collect only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this Policy — for example, contact-form messages are kept only long enough to respond to your query, and usage/analytics data is generally aggregated and anonymised over time.

Your Rights

You can ask us to:

  • Tell you what personal data (if any) we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate information.
  • Delete personal information you've previously shared with us, such as a contact-form submission.

To exercise any of these rights, reach out via our Contact Us page or email us directly — see the Contact section below.

Children's Privacy

Our Service is intended for job-seekers generally aged 18 and above, in line with the minimum eligibility age for most government recruitment. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we'll remove it.

External & Official Links

Valley Desk links out to official department websites, PDFs, and notifications (JKSSB, JKPSC, JK Police, J&K Bank and others) so you can verify and apply directly. Once you leave our Service through one of these links, this Privacy Policy no longer applies — we encourage you to review the privacy practices of any external site you visit.

Security of Your Data

We take reasonable, industry-standard measures to protect the limited data we hold. That said, no method of transmission over the internet is ever 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page along with a revised "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Contact Us

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Reach out any time:

hello@valleydesk.in
Or use our Contact Us page — we typically reply within 24–48 hours.
